Antarctica · EPSG:3031

Antarctica, seen with the South Pole at the centre.

The continent that Mercator tears apart stands in its true shape. Our server draws the SCAR GeoMAP geology itself.

Zoom in and the locations of KOPRI's rock samples appear. This is Victoria Land, home of Jang Bogo Station.

Dronning Maud Land, with the Norwegian Polar Institute's 1:250,000 geology and structural lines.

With the land beneath the ice sheet (IBCSO) as the basemap, add Antarctic stations and meteorite finds.

Geology SCAR GeoMAP · NPI · Sample, station and meteorite locations KOPRI KPDC · Basemap PGC REMA · IBCSO

Greenland · EPSG:3413

Greenland, with the GEUS 1:500,000 geological map.

The Geological Survey of Denmark and Greenland (GEUS) map — East Greenland's sedimentary basins stand out in colour.

GEUS stream-sediment geochemistry switches on in the same view.

Overlay dating sites from the Greenland government portal and KOPRI's samples.

Geology and geochemistry GEUS · Dating Greenland government portal · Sample locations KOPRI KPDC · Basemap PGC ArcticDEM

Svalbard · home of Dasan Station

Svalbard, with the Norwegian Polar Institute's geological maps.

NPI's geological units and faults come straight through, with KOPRI's rock samples on top.

The printed paper maps are there too, hill shading and all.

Ny-Ålesund, home of Dasan Station — where the glacier fronts retreated from 1936 to 2025.

Jan Mayen — a volcanic island in the North Atlantic. We take NPI's geology as shapes and draw it ourselves.

Geology and glaciers NPI · Sample locations KOPRI KPDC · Basemap NPI Sentinel-2 · PGC ArcticDEM

The poles, together

Scattered polar data, in one place

  • Antarctica — SCAR GeoMAP, IBCSO bathymetry, Dronning Maud Land (NPI), stations and meteorite finds
  • Greenland — GEUS geology and geochemistry, samples and mineral occurrences from the government portal
  • Svalbard & Jan Mayen — NPI geology, map sheets and glacier front changes
  • Arctic Ocean — KPDC observation data

Each agency's projection and server, aligned into one polar stereographic (3031 · 3413).

Tools for the field

Mark, measure, keep.

  1. Points — note the latitude and longitude where you click, and save them as a list.
  2. Distance and elevation — draw a line and an elevation graph appears with the distance.
  3. Area — outline a polygon and its area appears in km² and ha.
  4. Save as image — download the current map as a PNG with scale bar, coordinates and sources.

Upload your CSV or GeoJSON and it lands on the map as a point set.

Many sources, one screen

Different agencies' maps, on a single screen.

  1. KIGAM — from 1:50,000, 1:250,000 and 1:1,000,000 geology to geochemical and marine geology maps, the thematic maps of the Geo Big Data Open Platform.
  2. VWorld (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport) — faults, hydrogeology, protected areas, boundaries: national spatial data in the same place.
  3. Geological Survey of Japan (GSJ) — the 1:200,000 seamless geological map.
  4. East Asia — Korean and Japanese geology join on one screen.

Plus GEUS · NPI · SCAR · USGS · NASA · Macrostrat …
